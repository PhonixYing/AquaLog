AquaLog – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-01-24

AquaLog is a private, offline app for aquarium and turtle water test logging. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is used.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in required.
  • No ads, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.
  • Your tanks, logs, and reminders stay on your device.
  • Notifications are used only if you enable reminders.
  • Network access is used only for App Store purchases/restores or when you open external links or email support.

Data Stored on Your Device

To provide core features, we store the following locally:

  • Tank profiles (name, type, volume, notes).
  • Water test logs and maintenance events.
  • Reminder settings and schedule state.
  • Purchase status (Pro entitlement) cached on device.
  • Exports you create (CSV), saved to the location you choose.

You can delete a tank in the app or remove all data by deleting the app.

Photos & Files

AquaLog does not access your photo library. CSV exports are generated only when you choose to export and are saved to the location you select.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive your payment information. Apple may process purchase history according to its own policies.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone, precise location).
  • No contacts, camera, microphone, or calendar access.
  • No advertising identifiers or behavioral tracking.

Optional Support Email

If you contact us at lidonghui11911@gmail.com, the email is sent by your mail app. You control what you share and can remove any information before sending.

Children’s Privacy

The app is rated 4+ and intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Your data stays on your device within the iOS sandbox. Please keep your device updated and protected.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above and publishing the new version at this URL.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy or this app, please contact: lidonghui11911@gmail.com

概要
